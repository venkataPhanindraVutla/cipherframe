Life of a recording

From capture to ready-to-watch, one stage at a time. Segment length, ladder and bitrates are examples; the real encoding defaults are decided at gate P8.

Step-by-step simulation of one recording: captured by the embedding app, uploaded in resumable parts, checked, encoded into a quality ladder, cut into aligned segments, encrypted, described by playlists, and made ready to watch.

Illustrative only. See architecture.md and encryption.md.
